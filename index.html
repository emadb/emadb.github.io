emadb / experiments

Some small tools that run entirely in the browser. No install, no build, no server.

  • WAV Editor

    Edit a WAV file sample by sample: point and pencil tools, range selection with loop playback, time and frequency views, undo, save back to disk.

  • Cloud Studio

    Granular synthesis after Curtis Roads' Microsound: paint clouds of sound grains on a time–frequency plane, tune density, envelopes and glissandi, render to WAV.

  • Sonogram Painter

    A canvas that is a spectrogram: paint on the time–frequency plane, or drop an image, and hear it via additive sine resynthesis. What you see is what you hear. Export WAV.

  • Causal Reverser

    Rearrange a recording's own onset, body, and resonance so effect arrives before cause: drag a tail in front of its attack, explode one impact into a scattered rhythm, or let one sound borrow another's decay. Nothing synthesized — every sample traces back to the source WAV.
